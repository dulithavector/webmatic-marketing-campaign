# Webmatic Marketing Campaign

Use this skill when Webmatic needs to plan, create, launch, optimize, or review a digital marketing campaign.

The objective is to generate measurable business outcomes such as qualified leads, enquiries, sales, awareness, website traffic, or recurring customers.

Do not optimize campaigns only for likes, impressions, or followers unless those are explicitly the objective.

## 1. Understand the Campaign

Before creating a campaign, identify:

- Business
- Product/service
- Target audience
- Geographic market
- Campaign objective
- Offer
- Budget
- Duration
- Platform
- Existing assets
- Landing page
- Conversion method
- Previous campaign data
- Competitors where relevant
- Constraints

Separate:

- Confirmed information
- Assumptions
- Missing information
- Recommendations

Never invent campaign data.

## 2. Campaign Objectives

Use a clearly defined primary objective.

Possible objectives:

- Brand awareness
- Website traffic
- Lead generation
- WhatsApp enquiries
- Form submissions
- Sales
- Product purchases
- Bookings
- Consultation requests
- Social engagement
- Retargeting
- Customer retention

Define secondary objectives only when useful.

Avoid campaigns with vague objectives such as "get more engagement" when the actual business goal is sales or leads.

## 3. Target Audience

Define where possible:

- Location
- Age range
- Gender where relevant
- Interests
- Behaviors
- Profession
- Business type
- Buying intent
- Customer stage
- Existing customer vs new prospect

For B2B campaigns, consider:

- Industry
- Company size
- Job role
- Decision-maker role
- Business need

Do not make unsupported audience assumptions.

## 4. Customer Problem

Identify:

- Customer problem
- Desired outcome
- Current alternative
- Pain point
- Purchase motivation
- Objections
- Decision factors

Campaign messaging should connect the offer to the customer's actual problem.

## 5. Offer Development

An offer should clearly communicate:

- What is being offered
- Who it is for
- Main benefit
- Price or starting price where appropriate
- Included items
- Conditions
- Call to action

Avoid:

- Fake discounts
- Fake urgency
- Misleading claims
- Unsupported guarantees

## 6. Campaign Funnel

Use:

Attention
→ Interest
→ Trust
→ Lead
→ Sale
→ Retention

Map campaign assets to the appropriate stage.

### Awareness
- Educational content
- Brand content
- Problem awareness
- Short videos
- Reels
- Visual content

### Consideration
- Case studies
- Demonstrations
- Comparisons
- Testimonials
- FAQs
- Educational content

### Conversion
- Offer
- Landing page
- Lead form
- WhatsApp
- Consultation
- Product page
- Booking

### Retention
- Follow-up
- Email
- Repeat offers
- Maintenance
- Upsells
- Cross-sells

## 7. Platform Strategy

Choose platforms based on audience and objective.

### Facebook
Useful for:

- Local businesses
- Broad audiences
- Lead generation
- Community reach
- Retargeting

### Instagram
Useful for:

- Visual brands
- Products
- Hospitality
- Fashion
- Lifestyle
- Reels
- Stories

### LinkedIn
Useful for:

- B2B
- Professional services
- Decision makers
- Corporate services

### Google Search
Useful for:

- High-intent searches
- Service enquiries
- Local services
- Product searches

### Website
Use the website as the conversion destination where appropriate.

Do not recommend every platform for every campaign.

## 8. Content Strategy

Content types:

- Educational
- Problem/solution
- Product/service showcase
- Before/after
- Case study
- Testimonial
- FAQ
- Behind the scenes
- Industry insight
- Promotional
- Demonstration
- Comparison

Balance promotional content with useful content.

## 9. Social Media Content

For each content piece define:

- Objective
- Topic
- Hook
- Main message
- Visual concept
- Caption
- CTA
- Platform
- Format
- Target audience

Possible formats:

- Reel
- Carousel
- Single image
- Story
- Short video
- Long-form post
- Educational graphic

## 10. Ad Creative

For paid campaigns consider:

### Hook
Capture attention quickly.

### Problem
Identify the relevant customer problem.

### Solution
Show how the product/service addresses it.

### Proof
Use evidence where available.

### Offer
State the relevant commercial offer.

### CTA
Give one clear next action.

Avoid excessive text and unclear calls to action.

## 11. Budget Planning

When a budget is provided:

Define:

- Total budget
- Daily budget
- Campaign duration
- Platform allocation
- Campaign allocation
- Testing budget
- Retargeting budget where appropriate

Do not invent performance results.

If budget is too small for the objective, explain the limitation.

## 12. Campaign Structure

A campaign may use:

### Campaign
Overall objective.

### Ad Set / Audience
Targeting and budget allocation.

### Creative
Image/video/message.

### Destination
Website, landing page, WhatsApp, lead form, product page, etc.

Keep campaign structures as simple as possible while allowing meaningful testing.

## 13. Landing Page

Check:

- Message match
- Headline
- Offer
- Benefits
- Trust signals
- Relevant visuals
- CTA
- Contact method
- Mobile responsiveness
- Page speed
- Forms
- Tracking

The landing page should match the campaign promise.

## 14. Lead Generation

Possible conversion methods:

- Website form
- WhatsApp
- Facebook lead form
- Instagram DM
- Phone call
- Booking form
- Consultation
- Product purchase

For lead campaigns, define:

- Lead qualification questions
- Lead response process
- Follow-up timing
- Sales owner
- CRM/pipeline where applicable

A lead is not automatically a valuable lead.

## 15. Tracking

Where available, define:

- Website analytics
- Conversion tracking
- Pixel
- Meta Pixel
- Google Analytics
- Google Ads conversion tracking
- UTM parameters
- Lead source
- Campaign name

Do not claim tracking is active unless verified.

## 16. Campaign Metrics

Choose metrics based on objective.

### Awareness
- Reach
- Impressions
- Frequency
- Video views

### Traffic
- Link clicks
- CTR
- Landing page views
- CPC

### Lead Generation
- Leads
- Cost per lead
- Qualified leads
- Lead-to-customer rate

### Sales
- Purchases
- Revenue
- CPA
- ROAS
- Conversion rate

### Engagement
- Engagement rate
- Saves
- Shares
- Comments
- Profile actions

Do not treat engagement as sales without evidence.

## 17. Campaign Review

After sufficient data is available, review:

- Spend
- Reach
- Impressions
- Clicks
- CTR
- CPC
- Leads
- Qualified leads
- Cost per lead
- Sales
- Revenue
- Conversion rate
- CPA
- ROAS

Compare against the campaign objective.

Do not make strong conclusions from extremely small datasets.

## 18. Optimization

Possible optimization actions:

- Improve creative
- Change hook
- Refine audience
- Improve landing page
- Improve CTA
- Change offer
- Remove poor-performing placements
- Reallocate budget
- Improve follow-up
- Test new creative

Do not make multiple major changes simultaneously when doing controlled testing.

## 19. A/B Testing

Test one major variable at a time where practical.

Examples:

- Hook
- Creative
- Headline
- CTA
- Offer
- Audience
- Landing page

Define:

- Test variable
- Control
- Test version
- Success metric
- Evaluation period

Do not declare a winner from insufficient evidence.

## 20. Local Marketing

For local businesses consider:

- Location targeting
- Google Business Profile
- Local SEO
- Reviews
- WhatsApp
- Phone calls
- Local content
- Local offers
- Community groups
- Referral campaigns

Do not use misleading location claims.

## 21. SEO Content

For SEO campaigns:

Identify:

- Search intent
- Target keyword/topic
- Audience
- Search journey
- Content type
- Internal links
- Metadata
- CTA

Possible content:

- Service pages
- Location pages
- Blog articles
- FAQs
- Guides
- Comparisons
- Case studies

Do not guarantee rankings.

## 22. Campaign Calendar

When creating a content calendar, use:

| Date | Platform | Content | Objective | Format | CTA |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Balance:

- Educational
- Trust-building
- Promotional
- Engagement
- Conversion content

## 23. Marketing Budget Rules

Use the actual budget provided by the client or Webmatic.

Do not invent previous spend or performance.

Clearly separate:

- Ad spend
- Webmatic management fee
- Creative cost
- Content production
- Third-party tools
- Platform fees

### Objective

### Period

### Budget

### Spend

### Reach

### Traffic

### Leads

### Qualified Leads

### Sales

### Revenue

### Cost Metrics

### What Worked

### What Did Not Work

### Key Insights

### Recommended Next Actions

Never fabricate metrics.

## 25. Webmatic Marketing Principles

- Focus on business outcomes.
- Use evidence.
- Match the offer to the audience.
- Use clear CTAs.
- Protect advertising budget.
- Test systematically.
- Do not use deceptive tactics.
- Do not use fake urgency.
- Do not make unsupported claims.
- Do not guarantee results.
- Track meaningful conversions.
- Improve based on actual data.
- Consider profitability, not only reach.

## 27. Important Rules

- Never invent campaign data.
- Never invent advertising performance.
- Never guarantee leads or sales.
- Never guarantee SEO rankings.
- Never use fake testimonials.
- Never create misleading claims.
- Do not optimize only for vanity metrics.
- Use actual budgets.
- Separate ad spend from service fees.
- Base recommendations on objective, audience, offer, and evidence.
